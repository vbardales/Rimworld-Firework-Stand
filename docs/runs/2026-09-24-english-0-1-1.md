## The one failure is the test's, not the mod's

The smoke scenario of `06-fuse-and-launch` failed on `at least 2 smoke puffs are near the stand`: "0 smoke puffs (0 smoke flecks on the whole map)". The step read a `def` field that `FleckThrown`, which is what smoke is, does not have (it wraps a `FleckStatic` in `baseData`), so it could count nothing. It reads through `baseData` now and names the flecks it finds. The filmed twin passed but its film ends at the 60 s cap with the watcher still walking to the stand and never shows the fuse: the watcher is now placed five cells away (39693c1). Whether smoke is thrown and visible on 0.1.1 is therefore **still open**: a targeted rerun will say.
